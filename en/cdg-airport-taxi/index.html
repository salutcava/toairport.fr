FAQ

CDG taxi:
your questions

How much is a taxi from CDG to Paris?+
€56 to the Right Bank (1st-4th, 8th-12th, 16th-20th arrondissements) and €65 to the Left Bank (5th-7th, 13th-15th). It is a flat fare set by law: the meter does not apply.
How much is a taxi from CDG to the Eiffel Tower?+
€65. The Eiffel Tower is in the 7th arrondissement, on the Left Bank.
Where do I meet my driver at CDG?+
Your driver waits in the arrivals area of your terminal with a sign showing your name. Give us your flight number: we track the landing, delays included, at no extra cost.
How do I recognise an official Paris taxi?+
An official taxi has a "TAXI PARISIEN" roof light, a meter and a licence number. Ignore drivers who approach you inside the terminal: they are not official taxis.
Is the fare the same at night?+
Yes. The flat fare is the same day and night, on weekends and public holidays.
