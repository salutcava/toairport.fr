How the fare works

The Seine
sets the price.

North of the river is the Right Bank, south is the Left Bank. Paris airport flat fares depend only on that: CDG is cheaper from the Right Bank, Orly from the Left Bank.

Not sure which bank your hotel is on? Type its address in the booking form: the fare appears instantly.

Good to know

Paris airport taxis:
what the fare covers

The flat fare only applies to addresses inside Paris. From the suburbs, or between Orly and CDG, the taxi runs on the meter.

Which bank is my hotel on?

The arrondissement number in your address tells you: 5, 6, 7, 13, 14 and 15 are on the Left Bank, all the others on the Right Bank.

  • Eiffel Tower, Saint-Germain, Latin Quarter, MontparnasseLeft Bank
  • Louvre, Marais, Champs-Élysées, Montmartre, OpéraRight Bank
  • Gare de Lyon, Gare du Nord, Gare de l'Est, Saint-LazareRight Bank
  • Gare Montparnasse, Gare d'AusterlitzLeft Bank

What can be added to the fare?

  • Immediate booking+€4
  • Advance booking+€7
  • Per passenger from the 5th+€5.50

Nothing else: no night surcharge, no luggage fee, no toll. Payment by card is accepted in every Paris taxi.

FAQ

Paris airport taxis:
your questions

How much is a taxi from CDG to Paris in 2026?+
€56 to the Right Bank (1st-4th, 8th-12th, 16th-20th arrondissements) and €65 to the Left Bank (5th-7th, 13th-15th). Same price in both directions, day and night.
How much is a taxi from Orly to Paris in 2026?+
€36 to the Left Bank (5th-7th, 13th-15th arrondissements) and €45 to the Right Bank (1st-4th, 8th-12th, 16th-20th). Same price in both directions, day and night.
Is the taxi fare higher at night?+
No. The airport flat fares are the same day and night, on weekends and public holidays.
Are tips included?+
The fare is all-inclusive. Tipping is not expected in Paris taxis; rounding up is common for good service.
Is there a flat fare between Orly and CDG?+
No. Between the two airports the taxi runs on the meter. See the Orly ↔ CDG page (in French).
