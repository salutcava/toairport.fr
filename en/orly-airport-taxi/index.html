FAQ

Orly taxi:
your questions

How much is a taxi from Orly to Paris?+
€36 to the Left Bank (5th-7th, 13th-15th arrondissements) and €45 to the Right Bank (all other arrondissements). It is a flat fare set by law: the meter does not apply.
How much is a taxi from Orly to the Eiffel Tower?+
€36. The Eiffel Tower is in the 7th arrondissement, on the Left Bank.
Where do I meet my driver at Orly?+
Your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. Give us your flight number: we track the landing, delays included, at no extra cost.
Can I pay by card?+
Yes. Every Paris taxi must accept card payment. Cash is also accepted and a receipt is available on request.
Is the fare the same at night?+
Yes. The flat fare is the same day and night, on weekends and public holidays.
